I keep cutting these a beat too early. A bow still lowering. A god half-turned into another god. A storm that hasn't decided yet whether it's going to break. For a long time I did it on instinct and couldn't have told you why.

Then I noticed I do it everywhere. I read Osho, I read Tolle; I keep circling non-duality and presence — the idea that the unfinished *now* is the only place anything real happens, and that we hurry past it toward endings that matter less than we think. When I stop a film one breath before the finish, that's the part I'm keeping. The rest is the part everyone already knows.

Kshyv and I made eight of them. He goes toward devotion and mythology; I go toward light, fire, elemental force. We were chasing different things and kept landing in the same place — wondering whether the *becoming* itself could be the story.

So none of them resolve. Each one runs a few seconds and stops right where it's supposed to pay off. The ending isn't hidden. I just never wanted to get there.
