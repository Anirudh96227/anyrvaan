For a couple of years I've been making small animations with code instead of a camera — one at a time, until one at a time turned into hundreds. They sorted themselves into four collections that don't obviously belong together: counters and charts, old computer screens rebuilt from memory, everyday apps faked to the pixel, and old sacred patterns slowed almost to stillness.

They look like four different projects. Underneath, they run on the same handful of habits, just aimed at very different things — and that's the part I most wanted to find out. If the way I animate a business chart can also draw a mandala or bring back a 1995 desktop, then it's a method I can trust. It held. Here they all are; watch them in whatever order you like.
